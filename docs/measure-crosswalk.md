# Measure crosswalk

How each measure on the dashboard relates to the public CMS definition, and where it's implemented in Python. Written from the public CMS measure documentation and my own review of the original Qlik logic. It describes that logic; it doesn't reproduce it.

**Source definitions:** CMS, *Hospital-Level Risk-Standardized Complication Rate (RSCR) Following Elective Primary Total Hip Arthroplasty (THA) and/or Total Knee Arthroplasty (TKA)*. See the [measure methodology page](https://www.cms.gov/Medicare/Quality-Initiatives-Patient-Assessment-Instruments/HospitalQualityInits/Measure-Methodology.html) and [QualityNet](https://qualitynet.cms.gov/) (Hospitals – Inpatient > Measures > Complications).

> **Observed rates, not risk-standardized.** The CMS RSCR runs each hospital through a hierarchical risk model: predicted complications ÷ expected complications × the national rate. This dashboard shows **observed** rates: complications ÷ surgeries. They show where complications happen, but they aren't the published RSCR. The data has none of the claims-based risk factors CMS uses, so the RSCR can't be calculated from it. For hospital comparisons, the dashboard adds an observed-vs-expected ratio adjusted for the two risk factors the data does have, age group and procedure, labeled as not the CMS rate.

## Measures

| Measure | CMS definition | Python implementation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Surgeries** (denominator for every rate) | Index admissions for elective primary THA and/or TKA | `build_stays` collapses records to one row per index stay; the denominator is the stays left after filtering (`filter_stays`) | **Correction 1.** The original counted records, and a patient with two complications has two records. The file has no patient ID, so a stay is identified by hospital, birth date, admission date and discharge date. |
| **Procedure filter** (hip / knee) | THA and TKA cohorts | `has_tha` / `has_tka` flags from the procedure counts; the numerator and denominator both come from the filtered stays | A stay with both procedures appears under both filters, and once under "All". The original had one knee rate whose denominator was hip admissions; it can't happen here because both halves of every rate come from the same filtered set. |
| **Exclusion-adjusted population** | CMS cohort exclusions. The source file also flags complications excluded because of COVID-19 (see the COVID-19 section of the 2022 measure updates report) | `population="adjusted"` drops stays marked excluded and ignores COVID-flagged complication records | The exclusion applies to the numerator and the denominator together. The original adjusted mortality rate dropped excluded stays from the denominator only. |
| **Complication rate** | Share of index admissions with at least one complication in its window (yes/no per patient) | `complication_<population>` flag per stay, summed ÷ stays | Patients with a complication, not complication records. |
| **Complications by type** | AMI, pneumonia, sepsis/septicemia/shock (7 days); surgical site bleeding, pulmonary embolism, death (30 days); mechanical complication, periprosthetic joint/wound infection (90 days) | `summarize` → `by_type` and `timing_by_type`: complication records within the filtered stays, split by the "occurred during index stay" flag | Records, not patients, so a patient can count under two types. The windows come from the source file's complication flag; the rebuild doesn't re-derive them from dates. |
| **Complication rate by type** | Each complication type is part of the measure's yes/no outcome | `rates_by_type`: surgeries with at least one record of the type ÷ surgeries, with a 95% Wilson interval | Per surgery, like the overall rate: two infection records on one stay count once. Replaces the original's separate formulas for each type × procedure × population. |
| **Mortality** | Death within 30 days of the index admission, one of the measure's complications | `death_<population>` flag per stay ÷ stays | Same population rules as every other rate. |
| **30-day complication readmission rate** | Not a CMS measure. CMS readmission measures are all-cause; this file only records readmissions for a complication | First complication readmission per stay, days = complication admit date − discharge date; counted if ≤ 30 ÷ stays | **Correction 2:** labeled "complication readmission". Day 30 counts and day 31 doesn't. The original also had "readmission" rates with no window, which counted every complication readmission up to 90 days; the rebuild only shows the 30-day version, plus the time-to-readmission chart. |
| **Time to complication readmission** | — | Histogram of days to first complication readmission per stay (0–7, 8–14, 15–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+) | Complications that happened during the index stay aren't readmissions. |
| **Average length of stay** | — (descriptive) | Discharge date − admission date, in days, averaged over stays | Same as the original, but averaged per stay rather than per record. |
| **Length of stay by complication status** | — (descriptive) | `los_by_status`: average length of stay for surgeries with no complication, a complication during the stay, or complications only after discharge | A stay with complications both during and after counts as "during". |
| **Age and age groups** | Cohort: Medicare fee-for-service beneficiaries 65 or older at admission | Age at the admission date, accounting for birthdays; groups Under 65, 65–69, 70–74, 75–79, 80–84, 85+ | The original calculated age at discharge. Its age bands left out exactly 65 and anyone 100 or older, and mislabeled the 90–94 band. Under 65 is kept as a group so data-quality problems stay visible. |
| **Hospital comparison** | — | Observed complication rate per hospital with a 95% Wilson confidence interval | The interval keeps small hospitals from being over-read. It isn't the CMS interval estimate, which comes from the risk model. |
| **Hip vs knee odds ratio** | — (not part of the CMS measure) | `hip_vs_knee_odds`: for each complication type and for any complication, a 2×2 table of hip vs knee surgeries with vs without it. OR = (a·d)/(b·c), 95% interval by Woolf's log method; 0.5 is added to every cell when one is zero | Unadjusted. Surgeries with both procedures are left out. Uses the hospital and population filters and ignores the procedure filter. The same 2×2 method as the original odds-ratio workbook, rebuilt on synthetic data. |
| **Observed vs expected (O/E) by hospital** | CMS compares predicted with expected complications, using a hierarchical model with patient risk factors from earlier claims. This is a simpler cousin of that idea | `hospital_table`: indirect standardization. Each age group × procedure (hip, knee, both) group's complication rate across all hospitals is applied to each hospital's own patients to get expected complications. O/E = observed ÷ expected; adjusted rate = O/E × overall rate. 95% interval by Byar's approximation to the Poisson | Adjusts for age and procedure only, the risk factors this data has. The reference is all hospitals in the file, not the national rate, and it isn't the CMS rate. Above 1.0 means more complications than that hospital's patient mix predicts. |

## Issues found in the original Qlik measures

Rebuilding the dashboard and reviewing the original formulas turned up six issues. The first two are deliberate changes to how the rebuild counts. The other four were errors in individual original formulas. None of them carried over, because the rebuild computes every rate from one filtered set of stays rather than from separate formulas for each combination. Each one has a test with a small made-up case (`tests/test_outcomes.py`).

| # | Issue in the original | In the rebuild | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rates counted records, not surgeries | One row per index stay | `test_multiple_complication_records_count_as_one_stay` |
| 2 | "Readmission" implied all-cause readmission | Labeled 30-day complication readmission | `test_thirty_day_readmission_window`, `test_readmission_window_includes_day_30_and_not_day_31` |
| 3 | Knee-only rates with a hip denominator (AMI); a knee complication rate whose denominator was filtered to hip stays | Numerator and denominator from the same procedure filter | `test_knee_rates_use_knee_surgeries_as_denominator` |
| 4 | Exclusion-adjusted mortality dropped excluded stays from the denominator but not the numerator | Exclusions apply to both | `test_adjusted_mortality_drops_excluded_stays_from_numerator` |
| 5 | Age calculated at discharge | Age at admission, as in the CMS cohort | `test_age_is_at_admission_not_discharge` |
| 6 | Age bands skipped exactly 65 and 100+, and one band was mislabeled | Bands cover every age | `test_age_groups_cover_boundary_ages` |
