Hip & Knee Replacement Outcomes

Complications, complication readmissions and length of stay after total hip (THA) and total knee (TKA) arthroplasty, following the CMS risk-standardized complication measure definitions.

Volume and complication rate by year

Bars: surgeries (left axis). Line: share with a complication (right axis); shaded band: 95% confidence interval for each year. Overlapping bands mean the change could easily be chance. 2020 volume reflects the COVID-19 pause in elective surgery, which began in March 2020; fewer surgeries also mean a wider band that year.

Observed complication rate by hospital: funnel plot

Each dot is a hospital, placed by its number of surgeries. The funnel shows where its rate would fall by chance alone if its true rate matched all hospitals combined (95% and 99.8% limits). It narrows as volume grows, so a small hospital needs a much larger difference to stand out. Not adjusted for patient mix.

How the measures are counted

  • Rates are per surgery. Patients with several complications appear on several records; records are collapsed to one row per index stay before computing rates.
  • Complication readmission is a readmission for one of the measure's complications. The data has no all-cause readmissions, so this is not the CMS all-cause readmission rate.
  • Exclusion-adjusted removes stays excluded from the CMS measure and complications flagged as excluded due to COVID-19.
  • Observed rates, not the CMS risk-standardized rate (RSCR). CMS adjusts each hospital's rate for patient risk factors taken from a year of earlier Medicare claims, which this data doesn't have. Rates here are complications ÷ surgeries, so hospitals with older or sicker patients look worse. The observed-vs-expected chart adjusts for age group and procedure only.
  • Odds ratios compare the odds of a complication after a hip replacement with the odds after a knee replacement (2×2 table, Woolf 95% interval; 0.5 is added to every cell when one is zero). The age-adjusted version pools one table per age group (Mantel-Haenszel, Robins-Breslow-Greenland interval).
  • Complication windows follow CMS: AMI, pneumonia and sepsis within 7 days; surgical site bleeding, pulmonary embolism and death within 30 days; mechanical complications and infection within 90 days.
